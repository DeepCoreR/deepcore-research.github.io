About Deepcore Research

Deepcore Research is a research organization specialized in breakthrough technologies (DeepTech).

We conduct targeted research across strategic domains: quantum, photonics, humanoid robotics, defense, advanced interconnects and next-generation materials.

Our goal is to contribute to the mastery of critical technology building blocks, with a special focus on sovereignty and strategic autonomy.

Our principles

Technical depth

Rigorous research, experimental validation and measurable outcomes.

Strategic orientation

Priority to technologies with major industrial and sovereign impact.

Long-term horizon

Work positioned over 5 to 15 year cycles.

Sovereignty

Contribution to reducing critical dependencies and building controlled value chains.
